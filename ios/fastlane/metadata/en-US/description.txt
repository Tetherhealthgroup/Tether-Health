Care that meets you where you are.

Tether Health offers small steps, practical support and no judgment, for the parts of your health you choose. Start as a guest and keep everything on your phone, or create an account with your email to keep your plan and entries with you.

PROGRAMS IN THIS VERSION

• BreatheFree (tobacco and nicotine): make a quit plan at your own pace. Choose a quit date, your reasons and your triggers, add the people who support you, and use a guided breathing exercise when a craving hits. The breathing exercise works offline.

• Heartwise (heart health): log blood pressure readings and cholesterol panels. Target ranges come from your clinician; the app never sets or grades them.

• Steady (diabetes and metabolic health): log glucose readings, record the target range your clinician gave you, and take a gentle energy reset after a difficult reading. No calorie counting, no judgment.

• ClearAir (respiratory health): record the asthma action plan your clinician gave you, do a daily zone check and log symptoms and peak flow.

YOUR DATA, YOUR CHOICE

• No ads.
• As a guest, what you enter stays on your phone.
• With an account, your quit plan, program entries and profile are stored with your account, so they are there when you sign in.
• Health information kept on your phone is stored encrypted.
• With an account, you can export your data, or delete your account and data, in the app under Settings & privacy, Your data.

IMPORTANT

Tether Health supports everyday self-care. It is not medical advice and does not replace care from your clinician. If you think you are having an emergency, call 911 or your local emergency number.
